## H2: Buying Guide

- **Room size**: Minimum 12x14 ft for a standard L-shape
- **Orientation**: Left-hand (LHS) or right-hand (RHS) based on room layout
- **Seating**: 5–6 seater for families; 7–8 for large gatherings
- **Material**: Fabric for comfort; leatherette for easy maintenance
- **Budget**: Rs.22,000 (basic) to Rs.1,10,000 (premium modular)

## H2: FAQ – L Shape Sofa

**Q: What room size is needed for an L-shape sofa?**
A: A minimum 12x14 ft room allows comfortable placement of a standard L-shape sofa.

**Q: What is the difference between LHS and RHS L-shape sofa?**
A: LHS means the longer section is on your left when facing the sofa; RHS is the opposite.

**Q: Which is better: fabric or leatherette L-shape sofa?**
A: Fabric is more comfortable; leatherette is easier to clean and maintain.

**Q: What is the price of an L-shape sofa in India 2026?**
A: Quality L-shape sofas range from Rs.25,000 to Rs.75,000 for most budgets.

**Q: Can an L-shape sofa fit in a small living room?**
A: Compact 5-seater L-shapes (under 7x5 ft footprint) can fit in rooms as small as 12x12 ft.
