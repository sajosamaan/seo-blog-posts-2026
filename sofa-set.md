## H2: FAQ – Sofa Set for Living Room

**Q: Which sofa set is best for a small living room in India?**
A: A compact 3-seater or 3+2 sofa set under 7 ft length works best for rooms under 12x14 ft.

**Q: What is the most durable sofa upholstery for Indian homes?**
A: Leatherette is easiest to maintain; fabric (cotton blend) is most comfortable.

**Q: What frame material is best for sofa sets?**
A: Solid sheesham or teak wood frames last 15+ years; metal frames are rust-resistant alternatives.

**Q: What is the average price of a good sofa set in India 2026?**
A: A quality 5-seater sofa set ranges from Rs.25,000 to Rs.60,000.

**Q: How do I choose the right sofa set color?**
A: Neutral tones (grey, beige, cream) offer versatility; choose darker shades for low-maintenance families.
