## H2: FAQ – Wooden Dining Table

**Q: Which wood is best for a dining table in India?**
A: Sheesham is the best all-rounder for Indian conditions; teak is premium but pricier.

**Q: What size dining table do I need for 6 people?**
A: A 5–6 ft long table comfortably seats 6 adults.

**Q: How do I maintain a wooden dining table?**
A: Wipe with a damp cloth, avoid direct sunlight, apply teak oil every 6 months for solid wood.

**Q: Is engineered wood good for dining tables?**
A: Yes, for 3–5 year usage; solid wood is better for longevity.

**Q: What is the price of a 6-seater sheesham wood dining table?**
A: Expect Rs.25,000–Rs.55,000 for a quality sheesham 6-seater.
