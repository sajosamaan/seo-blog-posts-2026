## H2: FAQ – Corner Sofa

**Q: What is the difference between a corner sofa and an L-shape sofa?**
A: They are essentially the same; corner sofa emphasises the corner-fitting design, while L-shape refers to the shape.

**Q: How do I measure for a corner sofa?**
A: Measure both arms of the corner wall; ensure 18 inches clearance on all sides for movement.

**Q: Which material is best for a corner sofa with kids?**
A: Leatherette or microfiber fabric are easiest to clean and most durable.

**Q: Can a corner sofa be split into separate pieces?**
A: Modular corner sofas can be separated and rearranged; fixed ones cannot.

**Q: What is the price of a good corner sofa in India?**
A: Rs.25,000–Rs.70,000 for quality fabric or leatherette corner sofas.
