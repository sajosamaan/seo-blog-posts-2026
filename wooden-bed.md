## H2: FAQ – Wooden Bed

**Q: Which wood is best for a bed in India?**
A: Sheesham (rosewood) is the best choice for Indian conditions: durable, termite-resistant, and affordably priced.

**Q: Is hydraulic storage bed better than box storage?**
A: Hydraulic storage is easier to access; box storage is more affordable but requires lifting the mattress manually.

**Q: What is the price of a queen size wooden bed with storage in India?**
A: Rs.22,000–Rs.55,000 for a quality sheesham queen bed with storage.

**Q: How long does a sheesham wood bed last?**
A: With proper care, a sheesham wood bed lasts 20–30 years.

**Q: What finish is best for a wooden bed?**
A: Natural oiled finish preserves wood grain; lacquered finish is easier to maintain.
