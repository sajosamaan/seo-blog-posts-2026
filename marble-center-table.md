## H2: Buying Guide

- **Natural vs Engineered**: Natural marble is unique but porous; engineered is more practical
- **Base material**: Brass/gold adds luxury; black metal is modern; wood is warm
- **Maintenance**: Seal natural marble annually; avoid acidic cleaners
- **Size**: 2.5–3.5 ft diameter for round; 3–4 ft length for rectangular
- **Budget**: Rs.8,000 (engineered) to Rs.1,50,000 (natural Italian)

## H2: FAQ – Marble Center Table

**Q: Is marble center table good for daily use?**
A: Engineered marble is excellent for daily use; natural marble requires coasters and regular sealing.

**Q: Which marble is best for center table in India?**
A: Makrana white marble for classic look; Rajasthan green for a unique statement.

**Q: How do I clean a marble center table?**
A: Use a damp cloth with mild soap; avoid vinegar, lemon, or abrasive cleaners.

**Q: What base is best for marble center table?**
A: Brass or gold legs for luxury; matte black metal for contemporary rooms; solid wood for warmth.

**Q: What is the price of a marble center table in India?**
A: Rs.12,000–Rs.50,000 for mid-range; Rs.80,000+ for natural Italian marble tables.
