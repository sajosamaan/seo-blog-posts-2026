## H2: Types of Sofa Come Beds

### 1. Fold-Out Sofa Come Bed
- Seat folds forward to reveal mattress below
- Thicker mattress possible (4–6 inches)
- Price: Rs.20,000–Rs.60,000

![Fold-Out Sofa Bed](https://images.pexels.com/photos/1454806/pexels-photo-1454806.jpeg)

### 2. Pull-Out Sofa Come Bed
- Hidden bed frame pulls out from under cushions
- Roomier sleeping surface
- Price: Rs.25,000–Rs.80,000

### 3. Click-Clack Sofa Come Bed
- Backrest reclines to flat position
- Simplest mechanism; most affordable
- Price: Rs.12,000–Rs.35,000

### 4. L-Shape Sofa Come Bed
- Chaise section converts to bed
- Great for corner rooms
- Price: Rs.30,000–Rs.80,000

## H2: Buying Guide

- **Mattress thickness**: Minimum 4 inches for comfortable sleep; 6 inches for regular use
- **Mechanism**: Fold-out for comfort; click-clack for budget; pull-out for roominess
- **Frame**: Metal frame is stronger; wooden frame is traditional
- **Upholstery**: Leatherette for easy cleaning; fabric for daily sofa use
- **Budget**: Rs.12,000 (basic click-clack) to Rs.80,000 (premium pull-out)

## H2: FAQ – Sofa Come Bed

**Q: Which sofa come bed is best for daily sleeping use?**
A: A pull-out sofa bed with 5–6 inch mattress is best for nightly use.

**Q: How do I convert a sofa come bed?**
A: Fold-out: lift seat cushions, pull forward. Click-clack: push backrest back. Pull-out: pull base forward.

**Q: Is sofa come bed comfortable for sleeping?**
A: With a 4+ inch foam mattress, yes. Thin mattress sofa beds are only for occasional guests.

**Q: What is the price of a good sofa come bed in India?**
A: Rs.20,000–Rs.45,000 gets a quality 3-seater sofa cum bed with decent mattress.

**Q: Can sofa come bed replace a regular bed?**
A: Yes, for studio apartments; choose a model with 5+ inch high-density foam mattress.
