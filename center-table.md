## H2: FAQ – Center Table for Living Room

**Q: What size center table is best for a standard Indian living room?**
A: A 3.5 ft x 2 ft table works well for rooms up to 12x14 ft.

**Q: Which material is most durable for a center table?**
A: Solid sheesham wood offers the best durability; engineered wood is a budget-friendly alternative.

**Q: Can I use a center table with storage in a small living room?**
A: Yes, center tables with lower shelves or drawers are perfect for compact spaces.

**Q: What is the average price of a good center table in India?**
A: Rs.8,000–Rs.20,000 covers most quality options; premium marble starts at Rs.25,000.

**Q: Are glass center tables safe for homes with children?**
A: Tempered glass is safe and shatter-resistant; avoid thin glass tops for homes with young kids.
